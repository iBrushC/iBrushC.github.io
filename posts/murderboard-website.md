I found myself with a bit of free time and wanted to make a website to create those intricate evidence boards (murder boards) that always show up in movies and TV shows. It was written by hand in pure HTML/CSS/JS and doesn't have much technical complexity, but it was a nice several-hour sprint to make something I've thought about for a few weeks.
