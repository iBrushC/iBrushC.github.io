Title: Selling 45,000 Blender Addons
Description: Making one of the top blender addons of all time.
Image: animextras.jpg
Tags: Venture
Date: January 2023
Content:
I started learning Blender when I was 15 to make assets for game development, but quickly found I liked 3D art more than game dev. Since then, I have been using it on-and-off and have gotten quite good at it. However, when learning animation, I saw a real problem.

<br><br>

I initially learned animation from Alan Becker's 12 Principles of Animation Series, and a point that kept coming up was <i>onion skinning</i>, or shadows of previous frames that showed motion. Blender had no native ability to do this, so I looked and found people had only made crude implementations which duplicated objects and assigned materials, no proper viewport rendering.

<br><br>

So, in about two days, I made the addon <i>AnimExtras</i>, which has now sold over 45,000 copies and generated five figures of revenue. I later adventured into more technically complex projects, adding more functionality like animation smearing and painterly rendering, which all did quite well too.

<br><br>

Really goes to show how the right product in the right market will do big numbers. Hopefully I'll go on to do bigger things in the future.
