Title: A Scouting App for First Robotics Competition
Description: Changing how games tracking works in FRC
Image: scouting_app.png
Tags: Project
Date: December 2022
Content:
Scouting in the First Robotics Competition (FRC) is often overlooked because it is tedious and boring. You have 3-6 people watch the games, mark how well everyone is doing, then compile it all so that when you have to select your final team you know you'll have the best people. Typically this is done on spreadsheets or real pencil and paper, however existing solutions weren't very good.

<br><br>

Taking performance notes on paper gets out of hand really quickly, and data analysis is hard. The main issue with having a shared spreadsheet is that most of the time, WiFi in the FRC venues is extremely bad since all the robots use it as well and it throttles the performance. So, in order to solve that, I created an app.

<br><br>

The app, called TigerScout, has the convenience of a spreadsheet but is also able to work flawlessly with poor WiFi. It gives a quick interface for people to store points and notes easily, then saves it to their local device. During intermissions, people can step outside and sync their data to the cloud once, allowing everything to be in one place. I also added graphs and performance metrics to easily see how well all the teams performed with filters and sorting.

<br><br>

It was used throughout the 2022-2023 season and was really cool, since most of the NC district teams were using it, and many times I got to connect with people over it and share data between teams. Always a fun feeling seeing something you created being used by someone you don't know.
